Pioneering Agri-tech AI

We are a team of data scientists and agronomists dedicated to protecting the global coffee supply chain through advanced machine learning and early disease detection.

💡 Our Mission

Empowering Farmers with Precision Agriculture

Coffee leaf diseases, particularly the devastating Coffee Leaf Rust, account for billions in agricultural losses annually. Smallholder farmers are often the most affected.

Our mission is to democratize laboratory-grade disease diagnostics. Using MobileNetV2 transfer learning, an AI lesion scan and simple advice in four languages, we've built a tool that needs nothing more than a phone camera. On leaves it has never seen it is 92% accurate, and 78% on field-like photos (rotated, re-lit, blurred, cluttered backgrounds).

⚙️ Under the Hood

Our Architecture

A deep dive into the machine learning architecture that powers CoffeeGuard AI.

📐

MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning

A network pretrained on 1.2 million ImageNet photos already understands edges, textures and spots. We use its 1,280-number embedding of the cropped leaf and three zoomed tiles, so small lesions aren't lost.

🌳

AI Lesion Scan

OpenCV colour segmentation finds every lesion inside the leaf, measures the percentage of leaf area affected and draws a heatmap, so farmers see where the damage is and how bad it is.

🤝

Trust Gates

Every photo is checked for blur, darkness, glare and size first; non-leaf images are rejected; and when the model is under 45% confident it says “Not sure” instead of guessing.

🖼️

Honest Testing

Our training photos include flipped copies of the same leaf, so we split the data by leaf — no copy of a test leaf is ever seen in training. We also test on “field-like” versions of each photo. The original 64×64-pixel SVM + XGBoost model fell to 28% on those; the new model holds 78%. Photos are processed in memory and never stored.
