✅ Best Practices for Photos

For the highest accuracy, please follow these guidelines when taking photos of coffee leaves.

  • ✓ Ensure the leaf is flat and well-lit.
  • ✓ Avoid harsh shadows or direct sun reflections.
  • ✓ Center the lesion or diseased area in the frame.
  • ✓ Standardize a plain background (like white paper or ground soil) if possible.

🛡️ Data Privacy

All images uploaded to CoffeeGuard AI are processed entirely in memory and immediately discarded. We do not store, distribute, or retain your crop images on our servers. Your agricultural data is 100% private.
