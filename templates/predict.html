    ✅ Best Practices for Photos

    CoffeeGuard checks every photo first and tells you exactly what to fix if it's too blurry, dark or small.

    • ✓ Ensure the leaf is flat and well-lit.
    • ✓ Avoid harsh shadows or direct sun reflections.
    • ✓ Fill most of the frame with one leaf.
    • ✓ For a whole plant, take 3–5 leaves from different branches.

    🧭 Why CoffeeGuard is different

    • ✓ Shows where the disease is and how much of the leaf it covers.
    • ✓ Gives one clear decision: treat now, treat this week, or watch.
    • ✓ Reads advice aloud in Hindi, Kannada and Malayalam.
    • ✓ Works on weak signal: compresses photos and queues them offline.
    • ✓ Says “Not sure” instead of guessing.

    🛡️ Data Privacy

    Photos are processed in memory and immediately discarded — never stored. The outbreak map is opt-in and keeps only the disease, severity and an approximate location (~1 km).
