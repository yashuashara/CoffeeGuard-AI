🔍 Disease Library

Diseases We Detect

Our model can classify coffee leaves into four categories with exceptional accuracy. Learn about each condition below.

Healthy

Healthy Leaf

Normal, healthy coffee leaf with vibrant green coloration. Shows optimal photosynthetic activity and no disease markers.

Rust

Coffee Leaf Rust

Caused by Hemileia vastatrix. Creates orange-yellow powdery lesions on the underside of leaves. Most devastating coffee disease globally.

Phoma

Phoma Leaf Spot

Caused by Phoma costarricensis fungus. Produces dark brown to black necrotic lesions, often with concentric ring patterns on leaves.

Miner

Leaf Miner

Caused by Leucoptera coffeella larvae. Creates serpentine mining patterns between leaf surfaces, drastically reducing photosynthetic area.
